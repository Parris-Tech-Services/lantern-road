# Lantern Road art production shot list

Task: LR-0042 — Generate and style-lock the Lantern Road AI art set

This file turns the approved house style into concrete generation targets. It is a production brief, not a source of new world canon. Names and descriptions come from the current canonical content; coordinates and geography remain governed by the map canon.

## Composition rules for location art

- single scene only; never a collage, map board or UI mock-up
- 16:9 or similarly wide establishing composition
- one strong focal landmark
- practical low-fantasy construction and materials
- subtle ink/gouache field-journal texture
- muted earth, olive, slate and parchment palette
- warm lantern light used sparingly as a focal accent
- readable at phone-card scale
- no baked labels, logos, UI, frames or hex grid
- no giant crowds; small figures only for scale and life
- weather and road wear should make the March feel inhabited and travelled

## Settlement batch

### Hearthwick

**Identity:** warmth before the road.

A modest road village with honest mud, hens, smoke, hedges, carts and a welcoming inn. The village should feel safe because it is small and human, not because it is wealthy or fortified.

**Hero shot:** late afternoon/dusk looking down the muddy road toward the inn, with warm windows, wet ruts and wilderness continuing beyond.

**Avoid:** storybook-perfect cottages, oversized fantasy architecture, festival colour.

### Greyfen Market

**Identity:** useful, busy, contested.

A frontier market town shaped by tolls, ferries, carts and arguments about who keeps the road functioning. Mud and commerce should coexist.

**Hero shot:** broad market square with timber halls, canvas awnings, carts and pack animals, with a ferry/toll crossing or river infrastructure visible beyond.

**Avoid:** grand capital-city scale, pristine guild architecture, carnival atmosphere.

### Candlemere

**Identity:** knowledge in damp stone.

Old archive buildings, narrow canals, bridges, vellum, lamplight and mist. The town should feel scholarly without becoming an ornate magical academy.

**Hero shot:** blue hour across a canal toward tall old stone archive buildings, amber windows reflected in water, wet cobbles and covered walks.

**Avoid:** floating towers, glowing runes everywhere, university-campus neatness.

### Alderwatch

**Identity:** holding the line with too little.

A hard timber warden post guarding the southern reach. Everything is functional, patched and maintained by tired people.

**Hero shot:** raised timber palisade and watchtower over rough frontier country, signal lanterns, stacked supplies, patched roofs and wind-bent vegetation.

**Avoid:** castle scale, parade-ground polish, excessive military heraldry.

### Blacksalt Crossing

**Identity:** river work, tar and secrets.

A leaning river settlement of ferries, pilings, ropes, barges and narrow alleys. It should feel rough and secretive but not criminal by default.

**Hero shot:** overcast dusk along the riverbank, rope ferry and dark water in foreground, leaning timber buildings and tarred docks with lantern reflections.

**Avoid:** pirate visual clichés, tropical port colour, giant seagoing ships.

## Adventure-site batch

### Watcher's Rest

Lonely stone wayhouse in open country. Old warden practicality, soot, weather logs, simple stew-room warmth. Show isolation more than decay.

### Saint Rhel's Shrine

Small roadside shrine beneath leaning pines. Blackened lamp niche, worn devotional stone, travellers' offerings, old soot and quiet shelter.

### Old Barrow Keep

Broken hill-fort with reopened chambers and iron doors that sit wrong in their frames. Show recently disturbed ancient masonry rather than generic haunted-castle spectacle.

### Broken Span

Collapsed older bridge: wet stone, snapped timbers, river or ravine below, chalk marks and improvised crossings. The player should understand the travel problem at a glance.

### Weeping Stones

Three leaning standing stones in open country, visibly damp with mineral water in dry weather. Strange because of one impossible detail, not because of magical VFX.

### Redwater Ferry

Practical rope ferry on a working crossing. Mud, rope tension, toll hut, weathered landing and river current should dominate.

### Moonmere Tower

Pale old tower above reeds, half-collapsed but still proud. Vertical landmark visible from a distance; reeds and water should establish the approach.

### Mosslight Ruins

Collapsed stonework in marsh, green lights and black candles. The green light should be small, eerie and local rather than neon fantasy glow.

### Hollowglass Cavern

Cave mouth and interior mineral panes that catch light like brittle glass. Wind and resonance implied through composition; avoid crystal-cave saturation.

### Pilgrim Ford

A subtle shallow crossing used by pilgrims and smugglers. It should look discoverable to an observant traveller but easy to miss from the main road.

### Smuggler's Cache

Reed-hidden stone hollow near wet ground. Small-scale, plausible drop site rather than treasure vault.

## Enemy silhouette batch

Enemy art should read instantly at approximately 80–120 px.

- **Road Brigand:** practical layered scavenged gear, close weapon, road-worn silhouette.
- **Brigand Archer:** lean ranged silhouette, hood/scarf, simple bow, no heroic ranger glamour.
- **Marsh Wolf:** wet, rangy, underfed frontier predator; recognisable wolf anatomy with local mud/reed context.
- **Bog Lurker:** low amphibious/reed-concealed silhouette; unsettling through posture and concealment, not tentacle excess.
- **Veil Acolyte:** human cult operative in plausible travelling clothing with one subtle Ashen Veil identifier; avoid ornate evil robes.
- **Ruin Wisp:** small uncanny light-form tied to old stone; restrained glow with clear silhouette.
- **Ford Leech:** oversized river parasite adapted to shallow crossings; wet, simple and immediately unpleasant.
