# Lantern Road — Product Vision

## Target

Lantern Road is a **compact premium-indie browser RPG**, not an AAA-scale game.

The clearest product identity is:

> **Roadwarden + Curious Expedition + a tabletop road campaign, designed to feel native in a phone browser.**

The aim is not maximum content volume. The aim is a small Grey March where choices, people, systems and atmosphere feel deliberate and interconnected.

## Design pillars

### 1. A dense road campaign, not a giant open world
The Grey March should feel worth learning. Revisit places, let routes and settlements change, and prefer depth over adding regions.

### 2. Choices leave visible marks
Important decisions should change dialogue, relationships, services, danger, routes, faction behaviour or endings. Hidden numbers alone are not enough.

### 3. The party are people, not stat blocks
Garrick, Mira, Oren and Brindle should remember events, react differently, form relationships, suffer consequences and become people the player cares about.

### 4. Tense systems with readable trade-offs
Travel, supplies, combat, equipment, injuries and time pressure should create interesting decisions without grind, opaque punishment or dominant no-brainer choices.

### 5. Phone-first atmosphere
The game should be comfortable one-handed, readable, responsive and evocative through coherent 2D art, restrained animation, sound and strong feedback.

## Non-goals

- No tactical-grid conversion.
- No giant open-world content treadmill.
- No “AAA” asset-volume target.
- No hundreds of generic procedural events replacing authored consequence.
- No heavy framework rewrite unless a documented decision proves it is necessary.
- No feature counted as finished because the state changed silently behind the UI.

## Quality bar

Agents can measure correctness, consistency, clarity, pacing signals, repetition, defects and regressions.

**Josh is the creative director and final authority on whether the game is actually fun, emotionally effective and premium-feeling.** AI agents should present evidence and recommendations, not claim an objective fun score.

## Reference games

Use these selectively, not as templates to clone:

- Roadwarden — travel, investigation, inventory and authored conversation.
- Citizen Sleeper — characters, clocks, relationships and narrative pressure.
- Wildermyth — persistent party change and campaign events.
- Curious Expedition 2 — expedition pressure and procedural travel stories.
- Battle Brothers — equipment/progression depth and systemic consequences.

Lantern Road should remain recognisably its own compact browser road campaign.
