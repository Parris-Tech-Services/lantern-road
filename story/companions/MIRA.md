# Mira Quickstep — Direct Conversation Pack

## Voice

Quick, dry, observant. Humour is both affection and armour.

She values freedom, truth and being allowed to own her decisions.

She resists institutional obedience, forced disclosure and promises made on her behalf.

---

# Greeting states

## Guarded

> “What did I do?”

If Leader says “Nothing”:

> “Then this is already suspicious.”

## Steady

> “You need a route, a lie spotted, or an opinion you’ll ignore?”

## Warming

> “There you are.”
>
> “I was worried I’d have to form opinions without supervision.”

## Trusting

> “Ask.”
>
> “I reserve the right to answer a better question.”

## Hurt

> “Before you ask: yes, it hurts.”
>
> “No, I’m not dying.”
>
> “Those are separate achievements.”

## Exhausted

> “If you need optimism, Brindle’s over there.”
>
> “If you need accuracy, sit down.”

# Recurring topics

## “What was your life before the road?”

Guarded:

> “Earlier.”

Leader may:

- accept;
- probe gently;
- pressure;
- change topic.

Warming:

> “Different roads.”
>
> “People who knew a different version of me.”

Trusting and old-name thread open:

> “You already know enough to make the dangerous question interesting.”

After **The Name Before the Road**:

- reclaimed-name outcome:
  > “Mira Quickstep is still true.”
  >
  > “So was Venn.”
- buried-name outcome:
  > “Some names are doors.”
  >
  > “I closed one.”
- shared-name outcome:
  > “I told you because I chose the audience.”

Repeat-safe:

> “Still curious?”
>
> “Good.”
>
> “Curiosity is healthier than entitlement.”

# Requests

## Treatment request

Leader:

> “Let Brindle look at that.”

### Warming/Trusting

> “Look, yes.”
>
> “No speeches.”

### Guarded

> “I can walk.”

Leader:

> “That’s not the standard.”

She may counter:

> “Fine. She checks it. I decide whether we spend supplies.”

This preserves autonomy.

## Rest request

Leader:

> “You’re done scouting tonight.”

If exhausted:

> “Finally, an order with charm.”

If Guarded and not badly tired:

> “Ask.”
>
> “Don’t decide my legs are finished without consulting them.”

Leader can rephrase as request, improving response.

## Risky plan request

Leader:

> “Take the reed path alone and signal us through.”

If route suits her and purpose is clear:

> “I’ll go.”
>
> “But I choose the line.”

If it exposes old identity/history:

> “No.”
>
> “Find another scout or another plan.”

High Trust does not erase this refusal.

# Advice pattern

## Leader asks “What would you do?”

Mira should often return the question after answering:

> “I’d keep two exits.”
>
> “What are you willing to lose?”

This reinforces Leader responsibility.
