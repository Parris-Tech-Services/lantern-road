# Lantern Road — Direct Companion Conversation Pack

**Task:** LR-0091  
**Owner:** Agent 2 — The Storyteller  
**Status:** Authoring only. Runtime, save-state, treatment mechanics and roster mechanics are owned elsewhere.

## Purpose

The Leader should be able to talk directly to Garrick, Mira, Oren and Brindle throughout the journey and get more than one-use exposition.

Each companion pack contains:

- condition-sensitive greetings;
- recurring topics about self, road, factions and other companions;
- pre-decision advice;
- post-decision callbacks;
- treatment/rest/risky-plan requests;
- acceptance, hesitation, refusal and counter-offer lines;
- repeat-safe topic variants;
- personal-arc hooks.

The active party remains **Leader + three active companions**. One companion may be reserve.

## Conversation priority

When opening a companion conversation, prefer this order:

1. urgent current-condition reaction;
2. new major-choice callback;
3. personal-arc state;
4. trust-sensitive greeting;
5. recurring topic hub.

Do not replay first-time lines after a topic has been discussed.

## Trust bands

These names are authoring shorthand only.

- **Guarded**: low Trust; companion gives the Leader less benefit of doubt.
- **Steady**: neutral/working relationship.
- **Warming**: positive relationship and more personal candour.
- **Trusting**: strong relationship; criticism may become more direct, not more obedient.

## Condition pressure

Current condition modifies voice, not personality.

- hurt Garrick minimises himself;
- exhausted Mira loses some humour and patience;
- hurt Oren dislikes being treated as fragile;
- exhausted Brindle may refuse emotional labour she would normally provide.

## Advice rule

Advice is not a hidden correct-answer system.

Companions should disagree according to values and current knowledge.

Where possible, the Leader can ask all currently present companions before making a major decision.

## Presence rule

Only active companions may interject as direct witnesses.

A reserve companion may later learn:

- public outcomes;
- party-debrief information;
- major world changes.

They must not know private dialogue they did not witness.
