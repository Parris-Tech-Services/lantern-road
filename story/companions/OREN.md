# Oren Thale — Direct Conversation Pack

## Voice

Precise, curious, self-correcting. Dry humour appears when he feels safe.

He values knowledge and responsibility.

He resists certainty unsupported by evidence and attempts to make expertise into obedience.

---

# Greeting states

## Guarded

> “Is this a question or a conclusion wearing a question’s coat?”

## Steady

> “Yes?”

## Warming

> “I have three answers.”
>
> “Unfortunately you have not asked anything yet.”

## Trusting

> “Ask the inconvenient version.”

## Hurt

> “I am injured.”
>
> “I am not transformed into glass.”

## Exhausted

> “I can still think.”
>
> “I am less confident everyone needs to hear the result.”

Steady:

> “A field decision hurt someone.”
>
> “The report called it reasonable.”

Warming/Trusting:

> “The part I cannot settle is that it may have been reasonable.”
>
> “Responsibility survives that sentence.”

- retracted:

  >
  > “I still don’t know if simpler means truer.”
- amended:
  > “The record is less clean now.”
  >
  > “Good.”

  > “For once the Archive is not the only shelf that matters.”

Repeat-safe:

> “Still the page?”
>
> “All right.”

# Requests

## Treatment request

Leader:

> “Brindle needs to check you.”

### Warming/Trusting

> “Check, yes.”
>
> “I object in advance to the word ‘rest.’”

### Guarded

> “I am capable of reporting symptoms myself.”

Counter-offer:

> “Ask me what hurts. Then decide whether this requires supplies.”

He resists infantilisation, not care itself.

## Rest request

Leader:

> “Sleep.”

Exhausted:

> “An admirably concise argument.”

Not exhausted:

> “On what evidence?”

Leader can answer:

> “You’ve reread the same line four times.”

Oren:

> “…Accepted.”

## Risky plan request

Leader:

> “Go into the cavern alone and verify the markings.”

If evidence gain justifies it and risk is bounded:

> “I can do that.”
>
> “Mira chooses the exit route.”

If risk is performative or unnecessary:

> “No.”
>
> “The experiment does not improve because the scholar is more endangered.”

# Advice after failure

Leader:

> “What did we miss?”

Oren:

> “Careful.”
>
> “Failure does not prove the decision was irrational.”
>
> “But it gives us new evidence.”

If Leader is self-blaming:

> “Responsibility is not improved by falsifying the past.”
