# Hearthwick Rumours

Hearthwick gossip should feel close to the ground.

People notice:

- who arrived;
- who turned back;
- whether Tessa had to make more stew;
- whether shrine traffic changed;
- whether the road feels safe before any official says it is.

---

## HW-01 — Lanterns after dark

**Source:** inn table  
**Reliability:** RELIABLE  
**Topic:** Lanterns on the Old Road  
**Condition:** quest unknown/offered

> “Lanterns moved by the Weeping Stones again last night.”
>
> “Too low for stars. Too clever for ghosts.”

**Repeat:**

> “Same lights. Same stones. Fewer carts willing to check.”

**Superseded by:** HW-02A / HW-02B.

---

## HW-02A — The lights stopped quietly

**Source:** flour carter  
**Reliability:** LIKELY  
**Topic:** Lanterns on the Old Road  
**Condition:** lantern outcome bargained

> “Road's open again.”
>
> “Heard the lantern men walked away instead of being carried.”
>
> “One of them asked me for honest work.”
>
> “I said flour sacks are honest enough to hurt.”

**Use:** proves a bargain has social texture without claiming everyone reformulates their life.
