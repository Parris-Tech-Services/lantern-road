# Alderwatch Rumours

Alderwatch rumours should be blunt and operational.

People care about:

- supplies;
- patrols;
- who is missing;
- what road is usable tonight, not in theory.

---

**Source:** off-duty Warden  
**Reliability:** RELIABLE  
**Topic:** Fever on the South Road  
**Condition:** medicine quest not resolved
