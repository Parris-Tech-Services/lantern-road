# Blacksalt Crossing — Recurring NPC Dialogue

Blacksalt should feel like a place where everyone knows official maps are incomplete.

Its three voices:

- **Nera Vale** understands leverage and controlled truth.
- **Tobin Reed** understands privacy as hospitality.
- **Vesk Harran** understands politics as the thing that keeps interrupting river work.

---

# Nera Vale
**Role:** Smuggler Broker  
**Faction:** Ashen Veil

## Voice

Amused, precise and rarely impressed by moral certainty.

Nera does not speak in riddles. She says clear things that reveal less than the listener hoped.

## Motive

Keep alternative routes alive and keep the Ashen Veil too useful to remove.

## Callback A — Ledger given to the Veil

> “Still no receipt.”
>
> Nera smiles with half her mouth.
>
> “I know. You were hoping.”

Then, serious:

> “The people named in that ledger have stopped assuming paper only belongs to them.”
>
> “That was worth something.”

**Repeat line**

> “The ledger is safe.”
>
> “No, I won't define safe.”

## Callback B — Ledger returned to Guild

> “Oswin got his paper back.”
>
> Nera rolls a coin across her knuckles.
>
> “I assume he rewarded your faith in institutional self-examination.”

**Repeat line**

> “How's the Guild's conscience?”
>
> “Still alphabetised?”

## Callback C — Mosslight brokered

> “Nobody has been arrested.”
>
> “Nobody has been stabbed.”
>
> “Two medicine bundles crossed on time.”
>
> Nera lifts her cup.
>
> “Naturally, everyone involved insists no agreement exists.”

## Callback D — Mosslight exposed

> “Three routes went dark.”
>
> “One deserved to.”
>
> Nera watches you carefully.
>
> “The other two were carrying people who do not get invited to policy meetings.”

**Repeat line**

> “The marsh remembers patrol boots.”

## Callback E — high Ashen Veil standing

> “People have started assuming I sent you.”
>
> Nera looks offended.
>
> “Please develop a worse reputation before this damages mine.”

## Callback F — low Ashen Veil standing

> “You can still drink here.”
>
> “You can still ask questions.”
>
> “What you cannot do is assume the first answer is the useful one.”

## Callback G — Mira publicly names herself Mira Venn

> Nera says “Venn” exactly once.
>
> Mira's eyes narrow.
>
> Nera: “You chose to make it true in public. I'm checking whether the sky fell.”
>
> Mira: “And?”
>
> Nera looks toward the window.
>
> “Inconclusive. It always looks like that here.”

## Callback H — Mira buries Venn

> “Quickstep.”
>
> Nera uses the road-name with deliberate care.
>
> “No ghosts came looking for you today.”
>
> Mira: “Comforting.”
>
> Nera: “I did not say none were asking.”

**Purpose**

Required pre-ending carry-forward for Mira can use one of these callbacks.

# Tobin Reed
**Role:** Dockside Host  
**Faction:** Ashen Veil

## Voice

Warm, loud and socially strategic.

Tobin protects private conversation by making the room feel public.

## Motive

Keep Blacksalt useful to people who need a room, a meal or ten minutes not being observed.

## Callback A — good Veil standing

> Tobin points to a table in the busiest part of the room.
>
> “Best seat in the house.”
>
> You look at the crowd.
>
> “Exactly.”
>
> “Nobody remembers what was said where everybody could hear it.”

## Callback B — poor Veil standing

> “You can sit anywhere.”
>
> Tobin smiles.
>
> “You'll notice everyone else has made the same choice.”

## Callback C — Mosslight brokered

> “Back room's quieter.”
>
> “Turns out people shout less when they don't expect every meeting to become a raid.”

## Callback D — Mosslight exposed

> Tobin replaces three mugs on a shelf.
>
> “Lost customers?”
>
> He considers the question.
>
> “Lost locations.”

## Callback E — Mira shared-boundary outcome

> Tobin reads Lio's note:
>
> **Lio Venn. No inherited accounts.**
>
> He nods.
>
> “Good sign.”
>
> Mira: “Because it's clear?”
>
> Tobin: “Because somebody finally wrote down what they aren't selling.”

# Vesk Harran
**Role:** Ferryman  
**Faction:** Gilt Caravan Guild

## Voice

Broad, literal and chronically irritated when institutions make the river harder than the river already is.

## Motive

Move people across safely, get paid fairly and avoid becoming a faction symbol.

## Callback A — Guild-favourable ledger outcome

> “Inspections are shorter.”
>
> Vesk unties the ferry.
>
> “Nobody says it's because of your ledger business.”
>
> “That means it's because of your ledger business.”

## Callback B — ledger outside Guild control

> “Three inspections yesterday.”
>
> “Same rope. Same boat. Same river.”
>
> Vesk spits over the side.
>
> “Apparently paperwork has tides now.”

## Callback C — high Gilt Caravan Guild standing

> “Guild says you're reliable.”
>
> Vesk pushes off.
>
> “River hasn't given an opinion.”

## Callback D — low Gilt Caravan Guild standing

> “You still pay the same fare.”
>
> Vesk looks toward the Guild clerk.
>
> “He hates that.”
>
> “Improves the crossing.”

## Callback E — Moonmere chart recovered

> Vesk studies the copied route line.
>
> “Old ferry landing's real.”
>
> “Bad bank. Strong current.”
>
> He hands the map back.
>
> “People confuse ‘used before’ with ‘good idea.’”

**Purpose**

Prevents historical evidence from automatically becoming authoritative truth.

## Callback F — Common Road direction

> “Everyone wants a say in the ferry.”
>
> Vesk leans on the pole.
>
> “Fine.”
>
> “When the rope snaps, I still want one person whose job is replacing the rope.”

**Purpose**

Keeps the shared-responsibility ending grounded in practical accountability.
