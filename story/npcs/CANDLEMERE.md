# Candlemere — Recurring NPC Dialogue

Candlemere should feel like the place where memory becomes infrastructure.

Its three voices:

- **Sen Marrow** is curiosity moving faster than caution.
- **Iven Holt** is evidence sharpened into procedure.
- **Alwen Reed** remembers the difference between preserving a thing and belonging to it.

---

# Sen Marrow
**Role:** Archivist  
**Faction:** Archive of Candlemere

## Voice

Precise, curious and delighted by evidence without becoming oblivious to consequences.

Sen asks follow-up questions faster than most people answer the first one.

## Motive

Recover enough of the Grey March's old knowledge that present-day decisions stop pretending history began yesterday.

## Callback A — Moonmere chart recovered

> Sen has already made three copies.
>
> “One for use.”
>
> “One for argument.”
>
> “One for the inevitable moment someone claims the first two were misread.”

**Repeat line**

> “The chart has acquired six annotations and two enemies.”
>
> “Excellent progress.”

## Callback B — Moonmere chart still missing / partial evidence only

> “A missing document is not evidence that nothing existed.”
>
> Sen points to a gap between two old route references.
>
> “It is evidence that I need more tea.”

**Repeat line**

> “Still missing.”
>
> “Still irritating.”

## Callback C — Oren report amended

> Sen reads the amended Hollowglass record slowly.
>
> “You changed the evidence.”
>
> Oren stiffens.
>
> Sen looks up.
>
> “Good.”
>
> “Evidence is allowed to improve.”

## Callback D — Oren gives Mara Kett the page / independent account

> “An account outside the Archive is still an account.”
>
> Sen pauses.
>
> “I dislike how radical that sentence sounds in this building.”

## Callback E — strong Archive standing

> “People here have started handing you things before asking whether you can read them.”
>
> Sen smiles.
>
> “This is how institutions become vulnerable.”

---

# Iven Holt
**Role:** Magister  
**Faction:** Archive of Candlemere

## Voice

Dry, legally exact and fond of questions whose wording makes evasion difficult.

Holt does not worship records. He distrusts claims that cannot survive being written down.

## Motive

Make power explain itself in durable form.

## Callback A — Ledger archived as evidence

> Holt taps the catalogue mark.
>
> “The Guild may dispute the interpretation.”
>
> “It may not dispute that the document exists.”
>
> He looks satisfied.
>
> “That is a smaller victory than people imagine.”
>
> “It is also why we number things.”

**Repeat line**

> “Still exists.”
>
> “Still inconvenient.”

## Callback B — Ledger returned to the Guild

> “I do not object to private custody in principle.”
>
> Holt adjusts his spectacles.
>
> “I object when the people most embarrassed by a document become the people deciding whether anyone else sees it.”

## Callback C — Ledger given to Ashen Veil

> “A secret can preserve truth.”
>
> Holt folds his hands.
>
> “It can also preserve lies.”
>
> “The difficulty is that both look identical from outside.”

## Callback D — high Archive standing

> “You have become unusually welcome in a building designed around controlled access.”
>
> Holt glances toward the stacks.
>
> “Please do not make me regret the irony.”

## Callback E — Oren retraction outcome

> Holt reads Oren's retraction.
>
> “Clear.”
>
> “Responsible.”
>
> “Possibly too clean.”
>
> Oren: “Mara said something similar.”
>
> Holt: “Then I am in excellent company.”

## Callback F — Oren amendment outcome

> “A record that cannot admit new evidence is not a record.”
>
> Holt signs the amendment.
>
> “It is a shrine to the person who wrote first.”

---

# Alwen Reed
**Role:** Shrine Warden  
**Faction:** Wardens of the Green March

## Voice

Patient, observant and quietly territorial about sacred/local spaces.

Alwen has no interest in institutional arguments that erase the people who sweep the floor afterward.

## Motive

Keep Candlemere's side shrine a living place rather than an exhibit.

## Callback A — Reliquary returned to Saint Rhel's Shrine

> “Good.”
>
> Alwen says it before greeting you.
>
> “Candlemere has enough objects people can only appreciate through glass.”

**Repeat line**

> “Elira sent word.”
>
> “Apparently the lamp has become crowded.”

## Callback B — Reliquary archived

> Alwen dusts the empty niche.
>
> “It is safer here than on the road.”
>
> A pause.
>
> “Safety is not the only reason people build shrines.”

## Callback C — high Warden standing

> “The Wardens are speaking well of you.”
>
> Alwen sets down the broom.
>
> “That should make it easier to get doors opened.”
>
> “Remember that someone still has to live behind them.”

## Callback D — Brindle personal arc completed

> Alwen reads Brindle's altered prayer.
>
> “Name the road you could not take.”
>
> She nods once.
>
> “Good.”
>
> “Too many prayers are written by people with spare horses.”

**Purpose**

Connects Brindle's spiritual development to someone who works at a shrine but is not simply another priestly voice.
