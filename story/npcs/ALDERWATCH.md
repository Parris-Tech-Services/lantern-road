# Alderwatch — Recurring NPC Dialogue

Alderwatch should make scarcity feel operational rather than abstract.

Its three voices:

- **Edda Briar** thinks in responsibility under pressure.
- **Fen Lark** thinks in what the road actually feels like underfoot.
- **Yor Dane** thinks in remaining quantities.

---

# Edda Briar
**Role:** Warden Captain  
**Faction:** Wardens of the Green March

## Voice

Controlled, practical and unwilling to romanticise either authority or rebellion.

Edda gives orders because delay can hurt people. She also knows orders can become habits.

## Motive

Keep Alderwatch alive with too few people, too many roads and no perfect information.

## Callback A — Medicine delivered in time

> Edda does not thank you first.
>
> “Yor has stopped counting who gets the last dose.”
>
> Then:
>
> “Thank you.”

**Repeat line**

> “The ward is quieter.”
>
> “That's the report that matters.”

## Callback B — Medicine late

> “It helped.”
>
> Edda says it before anger can become the whole conversation.
>
> “People also waited longer than they should have.”
>
> “Both are true.”

## Callback C — Medicine diverted or failed

> “I know why roads fail.”
>
> Edda's voice stays level.
>
> “Flood. Ambush. Broken axle. Bad judgement.”
>
> “Only one of those can hear me now.”

**Repeat line**

> “We adjusted.”
>
> “Do not mistake adjustment for absence of cost.”

## Callback D — Mosslight exposed to Wardens

> “We closed two routes and made one safe enough for daylight traffic.”
>
> Edda rubs mud from a map edge.
>
> “Nera will tell you we strangled three useful paths.”
>
> “She is not entirely wrong.”

## Callback E — Mosslight brokered

> “I know there's an agreement.”
>
> “Nera knows I know.”
>
> “We are both pretending that sentence has fewer verbs.”
>
> Edda looks at you.
>
> “If it keeps medicine moving, I can tolerate some grammar.”

## Callback F — Garrick Shared Watch

> “Vale finally teaching people instead of volunteering to become architecture?”
>
> Edda gives him a brief nod.
>
> “About time.”

# Fen Lark
**Role:** Scout  
**Faction:** Wardens of the Green March

## Voice

Young, sharp and a little too accustomed to sounding older than he is.

Fen notices weather, tracks and tiny practical absurdities.

## Motive

Become competent without becoming numb.

## Callback A — Warden standing high

> “Captain says I can trust your route reports.”
>
> Fen adjusts his cloak.
>
> “No pressure.”
>
> “Only means if you're wrong I get wet somewhere more dangerous.”

## Callback B — Warden standing low

> “Captain says not to rely on your judgement.”
>
> Fen looks down the road.
>
> “She didn't say I had to stop using my own.”
>
> “What did you actually see out there?”

**Purpose**

Shows faction tension without turning people into reputation vending machines.

## Callback C — Mosslight exposed

> “Found three cut signs in the reeds.”
>
> “Wouldn't have noticed them before.”
>
> Fen's satisfaction fades.
>
> “Also found one abandoned food cache.”
>
> “Hard to know whether that's victory from the footprints.”

## Callback D — Mosslight brokered

> “Someone moved a warning marker six feet east.”
>
> Fen grins.
>
> “That's how the Veil says hello without admitting they were there.”

## Callback E — Brindle chose the scout

**Condition intent**

- If Fen is used as the scout in Brindle's authored arc, or implementation maps that role to another Warden.

> Fen has clearly been told not to make the situation awkward.
>
> “So.”
>
> He scratches behind one ear.
>
> “Apparently being strategically useful is very embarrassing.”
>
> Brindle: “You were also a person.”
>
> Fen: “Worse. Now I have two categories.”

## Callback F — Garrick Held Line

> “Vale, do you know scouts have a technical term for people who stand in one dangerous place too long?”
>
> Garrick: “No.”
>
> Fen: “Target.”
>
> Garrick almost smiles.

# Yor Dane
**Role:** Quartermaster  
**Faction:** Wardens of the Green March

## Voice

Terse, numerical and compassionate through logistics rather than sentiment.

Yor knows quantities because quantities are where promises become real.

## Motive

Keep Alderwatch from discovering too late that “almost enough” is still not enough.

## Callback A — Medicine delivered in time

> Yor checks the seal, checks the count, then checks it again.
>
> “Enough.”
>
> He stops.
>
> “Sorry. I don't get to say that word often.”

**Repeat line**

> “Still enough for this week.”

## Callback B — Medicine late

> “Late stock is still stock.”
>
> Yor writes the arrival time down.
>
> “The people who waited do not care that my ledger has a category for that.”

## Callback C — Medicine diverted

> Yor looks at the empty shelf.
>
> “I already counted what didn't arrive.”
>
> “You don't need to help.”

**Repeat line**

> “If you have supplies to sell, say so.”
>
> “If you have an explanation, Crow likes those more than I do.”

## Callback D — high Warden standing

> “Standing doesn't make bandages multiply.”
>
> Yor hands over the requisition slate.
>
> “It does mean I believe you when you say a road is worth sending them down.”

## Callback E — low Warden standing

> “I can sell you what the rules allow.”
>
> “Trust and inventory are separate ledgers.”
>
> Yor looks up.
>
> “Today.”

**Purpose**

Makes standing legible without making it magical.
