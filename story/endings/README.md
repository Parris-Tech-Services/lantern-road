# Lantern Road — Modular Ending Prose Pack

**Task:** LR-0076 — Author modular campaign epilogues and ending prose  
**Owner:** Agent 2 — The Storyteller  
**Status:** Authoring only. LR-0008 later integrates this copy into the campaign runtime.

## Purpose

LR-0043 established the 18-day campaign structure and ending matrix.

LR-0076 supplies the finished player-facing prose that the later ending assembler can combine.

An ending should feel specific to what the player actually did rather than like a generic victory paragraph with numbers inserted.

## Assembly order

Recommended sequence:

1. **Regional ending family**
2. **Legitimacy variant**
3. **Two to four strongest quest callbacks**
4. **Faction posture callback if materially relevant**
5. **Party epilogue blocks**
6. **One unresolved-thread block if applicable**
7. **Final image/line**

Do not print every possible callback in one ending.

A premium-feeling epilogue should be selective enough to read like a story, while still proving the game remembered the player.

## Regional ending families

- The Gilded Road
- The Guarded Road
- The Recorded Road
- The Many Roads
- The Common Road
- The Fractured March

## Legitimacy

Use the approved design intent:

- **High legitimacy:** the party has enough Renown and cross-region credibility that its final position meaningfully shapes the regional settlement.
- **Low legitimacy:** the party can influence local details or endorse a direction, but institutions act largely on their own terms.

Do not use Renown as a morality score.

## Callback selection

Prioritise callbacks that:

1. involved a real choice;
2. caused visible harm/help;
3. affected the chosen regional ending;
4. involved a party member's personal arc;
5. remain unresolved.

Avoid recounting all six quests mechanically.
