# Ending Assembly and Condition Notes

This file describes how LR-0008 should later select authored blocks.

It is not runtime code.

## Minimum ending

Every ending should include:

1. one regional family block;
2. one legitimacy variant;
3. at least two named-history callbacks if available;
4. at least one party block;
5. final line/image.

## Callback ranking

Suggested ranking score conceptually:

1. final-ending relevance;
2. major human consequence;
3. unresolved harm;
4. personal arc completion;
5. strong faction conflict;
6. recent memorable choice.

Do not expose a score.

## Avoid contradictory blocks

Examples:

- Do not show “medicine delivered in time” and “medicine too late.”
- Do not show both shrine and Archive reliquary custody.
- Do not show multiple Mira personal outcomes.
- If a quest is unresolved, prefer the unresolved block rather than guessing the intended outcome.

## High/low legitimacy

The regional file already contains high/low prose.

LR-0008 should not merely prepend:

> Because you had enough Renown...

The difference should be visible in what institutions accepted.

## Common Road guardrail

The Common Road must include:

- at least one explicit cost;
- at least one dissatisfied faction voice or consequence;
- no claim that the March is “united” or permanently solved.

## Fractured March guardrail

Fractured March must still include local success callbacks.

Do not turn it into:

> You failed to reach 12 Renown. Game over.

## Unresolved quest guardrail

Unresolved content should use known uncertainty.

Bad:

> The ledger was eventually destroyed.

Good:

> The ledger remains absent, and people continue arguing about what it contained.

## Personal arc guardrail

If LR-0044 remains unintegrated or a personal quest was never started, use the generic Trust variant.

Do not invent a personal resolution.

## Suggested closing-line mapping

- Gilded Road: **“By winter, the road is easier to price than it is to argue with.”**
- Guarded Road: **“At dusk, more lanterns burn beside the road. Not all of them are there to welcome travellers.”**
- Recorded Road: **“The new map has margins wide enough for corrections. It will need them.”**
- Many Roads: **“The road on the official map ends at the reeds. People keep walking.”**
- Common Road: **“Nobody owns the road. Everybody has work to do.”**
- Fractured March: **“The Grey March still has roads. It no longer agrees where they lead.”**
