# Grey March Generated-Label Reconciliation

**Task:** LR-0071  
**Owner:** Agent 2 — The Storyteller  
**Authority:** Proposal only. Agent 7 owns map canon; Josh remains final creative authority.

## Rule

The first Grey March concept map is approved as a **visual direction**, not as a source of automatic place canon.

This audit classifies each generated regional label as:

- **ADOPT** — recommend making the name canonical as a non-node regional label;
- **RENAME** — the geography/lore idea can work, but the generated name should change;
- **REJECT** — the label adds confusion or redundant geography without enough benefit;
- **HOLD FOR JOSH** — the label requires a genuine creative/geographic decision rather than routine reconciliation.

No classification in this file moves a canonical settlement/site, assigns a new gameplay coordinate or edits `world/map-canon.json`.

# Label-by-label audit

## 1. Ashen Ridge → RENAME: Barrow Ridge

### Conflict

The word **Ashen** already carries strong identity through the **Ashen Veil**.

Using “Ashen Ridge” on the regional map risks one of three false implications:

- the Ashen Veil originated there;
- the faction controls the ridge;
- “ashen” is a broader cultural/geographic identity connected to the faction.

None of those is established.

### Better anchor

**Old Barrow Keep** already occupies the central-eastern high ground at F4.

A regional **Barrow Ridge** can be a modest line of raised, stony country around the keep rather than a new destination.

### Recommendation

**RENAME → Barrow Ridge**

This should remain PROPOSED until Agent 7 ratifies it.

---

## 2. Duskwood → RENAME: Watcherwood

### Conflict

“Duskwood” has no existing story anchor and reads as broad fantasy atmosphere rather than something the people of the Grey March would specifically call their local woodland.

### Existing support

The western/north-western side of the grid contains several forest tiles near the approach to **Watcher's Rest**.

Saint Rhel's Shrine is also already described as standing under leaning pines farther south.

### Recommendation

**RENAME → Watcherwood**

The name implies country known through the old Warden wayhouse rather than inventing a wholly separate forest culture.

Do not make Watcher's Rest the political owner of the wood; it is merely the recognisable anchor.

---

## 3. Mourn Lake → HOLD FOR JOSH

### Conflict

Map Canon v1 currently contains:

- plains;
- hills;
- forest;
- mountain;
- swamp;
- river.

It contains **no lake terrain**.

The concept painting may visually benefit from a lake, and Josh explicitly liked broad land with lakes/forests/villages as an art direction. But turning a painted lake into named canon would require a controlled geography decision.

### Recommendation

**HOLD FOR JOSH**

Possible future questions:

- Is the lake inside the playable 9×8 Grey March?
- Is it outside the playable grid as backdrop?
- Does it connect to the Redwater river system?
- Is Moonmere Tower named for this body of water or something else?

Do not answer those by accident in Storyteller prose.

---

## 4. Hollowwold → ADOPT

### Existing support

**Hollowglass Cavern** at E2 already gives the north-central interior a distinctive physical motif: brittle mineral panes that ring in changing wind.

The surrounding area includes hills, forest and plains rather than one uniform biome, which suits the older meaning of *wold* as open/rising country better than treating “Hollowwold” as another forest.

### Recommendation

**ADOPT: Hollowwold**

Suggested use:

> the broken upland and wind-cut country around Hollowglass Cavern

It is a landscape name, not a settlement.

It must not create a new node or require Hollowglass Cavern to move.

---

## 5. Reedmarsh → ADOPT

### Existing support

The southern Grey March already repeatedly uses:

- **southern reeds**;
- marsh routes;
- Mosslight lights;
- Pilgrim Ford;
- Redwater Ferry;
- Blacksalt Crossing;
- Smuggler's Cache;
- explicit swamp terrain.

These currently feel like one connected human/geographic problem without a concise regional name.

### Recommendation

**ADOPT: Reedmarsh**

Suggested use:

> the low southern river-and-reed country whose official crossings and unofficial paths overlap

This is particularly useful because the campaign repeatedly contrasts:

- official road;
- ferry;
- ford;
- hidden reed path;
- Veil route.

A single regional name helps the player learn one dense place rather than memorise disconnected “marsh content.”

---

## 6. Embermere → REJECT

### Conflicts

- No lake/mere is currently canonical.
- **Moonmere Tower** already owns the most prominent “mere” sound in the setting.
- An “Embermere” invites players to expect a second significant body of water with lore, routes and relevance.

That creates breadth without current payoff.

### Recommendation

**REJECT**

If the concept painting needs a warm-coloured body of water visually, leave it unlabeled until a controlled geography decision gives it a reason to exist.

---

## 7. Siltbrook Marsh → REJECT

### Conflicts

No **Siltbrook** currently appears in canon.

The southern river identity already includes **Redwater Ferry**, and the proposed **Reedmarsh** cleanly covers the surrounding swamp/reed country.

Adding “Siltbrook Marsh” would produce multiple near-synonymous marsh regions in a campaign whose strength is learnability.

### Recommendation

**REJECT**

Do not create a second marsh merely because the painting contains another patch of wet terrain.

---

## 8. Wyrthen Forest → HOLD FOR JOSH

### Opportunity

“Wyrthen” is distinctive enough to be useful.

A named forest could make the illustrated map easier to talk about.

### Problem

The current canonical forest tiles form several separated patches rather than one obvious single forest region.

Without the concept painting's exact label placement being part of the canonical registry, Storyteller work cannot responsibly decide which patch “Wyrthen Forest” means.

### Recommendation

**HOLD FOR JOSH**

If Josh/Agent 7 confirms a coherent visual forest mass corresponds to an existing set of forest hexes, the name can be reconsidered without moving any places.

---

## 9. Blackfen Plains → RENAME: Greyfen Plain

### Conflict

“Fen” means wet/marshy ground while the central Greyfen road corridor is predominantly **plains**.

The prefix **Black-** also creates unnecessary competition with **Blacksalt Crossing**.

### Existing anchor

**Greyfen Market** is already the central road/market town.

It is natural for surrounding open country to be referred to as the **Greyfen Plain**.

### Recommendation

**RENAME → Greyfen Plain**

Keep it singular and local-sounding.

This makes Greyfen Market feel embedded in landscape rather than named randomly.

---

## 10. Stoneveil Mountains → RENAME: Stoneveil Heights

### Existing support

Mountain and hill terrain exists along the northern edge of Map Canon v1.

A strong northern silhouette is also part of the approved visual direction.

### Scope risk

“Stoneveil Mountains” sounds like a large separate mountain region the player ought to explore.

Lantern Road is explicitly not building a giant open world.

### Recommendation

**RENAME → Stoneveil Heights**

Use it as the northern high-country boundary/horizon, with existing mountain/hill hexes representing its reachable lower country.

It should not imply a new mountain campaign beyond the map.
