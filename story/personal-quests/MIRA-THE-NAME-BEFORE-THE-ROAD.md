# Mira Quickstep — The Name Before the Road

## Character question

> **When does freedom become merely leaving the consequences to someone else?**

Mira is good at exits.

Her personal arc should not punish her for surviving, using aliases or distrusting institutions.

It should ask what happens when the identity she discarded still has consequences for somebody who could not leave as easily.

## Entry requirements

Declarative intent:

- Mira Trust at least **Trusting** or equivalent threshold.
- Memory `mira_old_name`.
- Prefer after the player has interacted with Blacksalt Crossing or the Ashen Veil.
- Strongest after the missing-ledger or Mosslight material has made secrecy politically meaningful.
- Ideal window: days 7–15.

## The old name

Mira's birth name is:

> **Mira Venn**

“Quickstep” began as a road-name used by runners who did not want ledgers following them from town to town.

She kept it because it was useful.

Then because it was safer.

Then because eventually fewer people knew the difference.

## Backstory

Years earlier, Mira Venn worked as a courier between Blacksalt, Pilgrim Ford and smaller unofficial crossings.

She was not formally Ashen Veil, but she moved in the same ecosystem.

A route broker named **Sella Venn**, Mira's older cousin, used family names to secure trust with people who would not deal with anonymous runners.

Mira discovered that Sella had started selling route timing to both smugglers and toll enforcers.

Mira exposed enough of the operation to break it, then disappeared before either side could decide whether she was witness, traitor or asset.

A younger courier named **Lio Venn** was left carrying the family name.

Lio is not innocent.

He kept running the routes.

But he inherited scrutiny that Mira escaped.

The arc is not “Mira must confess and be forgiven.”

The real trade-off is whether a buried identity belongs only to the person who buried it when other people are still living under its consequences.

---

# Beat 1 — Someone Uses the Wrong Name

At Blacksalt, Tobin Reed or Vesk hears another traveller call Mira:

> “Venn?”

Mira answers before thinking:

> “Wrong woman.”

The traveller apologises.

Mira is visibly unsettled.

## Player choices

### Ask privately

Mira admits “Quickstep” is not the first name people used for her on the road.

She does not yet tell the whole story.

### Let it go

Later, Nera Vale brings it up instead.

Mira remembers that the player did not press.

### Joke about secret identities

Only works at high Trust.

Mira:

> “Please. If I had a dramatic secret identity I'd have chosen a surname with better boots.”

Then:

> “Venn.”

The humour should break tension, not erase it.

---

# Beat 2 — Lio's Mark

At Pilgrim Ford or a nearby route marker, Mira recognises a cut-sign used by her old courier network.

The mark means:

> **Venn debt carried forward.**

Lio has been using the name to keep a route open.

That gives him access.

It also means people still collect against Mira's unfinished history.

## Nera's offer

Nera can make the problem disappear in one of two ways:

1. spread word that Mira Venn died years ago;
2. publish enough truth through the Veil network that people stop treating Lio as collateral for Mira.

Nera dislikes the second option because truth cannot be controlled once released.

## Mira's preference

Mira initially prefers the death story.

> “Dead people are wonderfully hard to invoice.”

If challenged, she becomes serious:

> “I know what truth costs when the wrong person owns the road.”

This is not cowardice. It is learned survival.

---

# Beat 3 — Lio Venn

Lio is younger than Mira but not a child.

He has spent years hearing contradictory versions of her:

- brave;
- selfish;
- smart;
- traitor;
- the reason one route stayed open;
- the reason another family lost work.

He does not need an apology performance.

He wants Mira to make one decision **while staying present for the consequences**.

Lio:

> “Pick a story. Just don't leave me to be the person standing beside it.”

This line should define the arc.

---

# Final choice

## Outcome A — **Bury Mira Venn**

Mira publicly confirms the false death story.

“Quickstep” becomes the only identity she will use.

Lio gets a clean separation from her old debts because the network is told he inherited nothing from a dead relative.

### Benefit

Mira keeps the safety and freedom she built.

Lio receives a practical break from her history.

### Cost

The truth becomes another controlled story maintained by people like Nera.

Mira chooses freedom through disappearance.

### Remembered outcome

`mira_outcome_buried_name`

### Required later callback

This outcome **must** surface later outside the immediate scene.

Examples:

- Nera calls her “Quickstep” with deliberate emphasis after a faction dispute.
- An ending line notes that no public record ever connects Mira Quickstep to the Venn routes.
- Lio sends a message addressed only to “Mira,” refusing both surnames.
- If the player supports the Archive, Mira remarks that some truths remain deliberately absent from the record.

### Ending direction

Strong tension with **The Recorded Road**.

Compatible with **The Many Roads**, but the ending must not pretend secrecy is costless.

Mira line:

> “A blank space can keep you alive. It can also let somebody else write whatever they like around it.”

---

## Outcome B — **Tell the Whole Story**

Mira gives Lio, Nera and a neutral witness the full account of Sella's route-selling and Mira's role in exposing it.

The story spreads.

### Benefit

Lio is no longer collateral for a mystery.

People can judge Mira's choices rather than the rumours around them.

### Cost

Mira becomes findable.

Old enemies and old obligations may now know exactly who survived.

### Remembered outcome

`mira_outcome_named_truth`

### Required later callback

At least one future NPC must call her **Mira Venn** in a context where the choice matters.

Mira's reaction depends on Trust:

High Trust:

> She flinches, then answers.

Lower Trust:

> She answers, but later asks why you looked at her when the name was said.

### World callback

A later route dispute can cite the Venn account as evidence that unofficial routes also need accountability.

### Ending direction

Especially resonant with **The Common Road** or **The Recorded Road** if those endings make room for testimony that does not begin in official records.

---

## Outcome C — **Let Lio Carry the Name, but Not the Debt**

Mira does not reclaim “Venn” publicly.

She also refuses the death story.

Instead she makes a witnessed statement that Lio's work is his own and that old claims against Mira do not transfer to him.

This is the messiest solution.

### Benefit

Lio keeps his name without pretending history vanished.

Mira keeps some separation from her old identity.

### Cost

Ambiguity remains. Some people will still connect them.

### Remembered outcome

`mira_outcome_shared_boundary`

### Required later callback

Lio later signs a route message:

> **Lio Venn. No inherited accounts.**

Mira keeps the scrap.

She pretends not to.

### Ending direction

Strong resonance with **The Common Road** because the choice establishes a boundary rather than total secrecy or total exposure.

---

# Resolution scene

Mira and the player walk away from Blacksalt or Pilgrim Ford.

She says:

> “You know what I hate about names?”
>
> “People think keeping one means you stayed the same person.”

The player can answer:

### “You can keep a name without owing it your whole life.”

Trust-positive.

### “You can leave a name and still owe people an answer.”

Harder, but Mira may respect it if the player has demonstrated consistency.

### “Quickstep suits you better.”

Warm but evasive.

Mira:

> “That's because I picked it.”

Then, after a beat:

> “That mattered.”

# Mandatory carry-forward requirement

LR-0044 acceptance explicitly requires Mira's choice to matter later.

LR-0037 integration must therefore implement **at least two** of the following:

1. **Later dialogue callback**
   - Nera, Lio, Tobin, Vesk or another relevant NPC uses or avoids “Venn” based on outcome.

2. **Relationship callback**
   - Mira reacts differently when another party member discusses records, secrecy or identity.

3. **World response**
   - a route or faction conversation cites the Venn account, the false death, or Lio's boundary statement.

4. **Ending callback**
   - the epilogue names whether Mira remained Quickstep alone, reclaimed Venn publicly, or left the name with Lio under explicit limits.

At least one callback must occur **before** the final ending so the choice does not feel deferred until credits.
