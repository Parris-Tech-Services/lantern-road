# Lantern Road — Camp Events

## C01 — Boots by the Fire

**Purpose:** Garrick character; Trust callback.  
**Repeat:** one-shot before/after personal arc with variant.  
**Intended conditions:**

- camp;
- Garrick present;
- preferably after difficult travel;
- variant by Garrick personal outcome.

### Setup

Garrick lines everyone's boots near the fire without asking.

He rotates them before the leather scorches.

### Branch A — help him

He initially says:

> “I've got it.”

If Trust is positive, the player can answer:

> “I know.”

Then help anyway.

### Branch B — ask why he always does this

> “Wet feet become slow feet.”
>
> “Slow feet become people making decisions because they're tired.”

If `shared_watch` outcome:

> He hands you Mira's left boot.
>
> “Turn that one.”

If `held_line` outcome:

> He keeps all four pairs.

If `closed_crossing` outcome:

> “Some things are worth stopping before they become heroic.”

## C02 — Mira Counts the Exits

**Purpose:** Mira character; location awareness; old-name callback.  
**Repeat:** one-shot with later variant.  
**Intended conditions:**

- camp near settlement or enclosed ruin;
- Mira present;
- Trust or old-name memory affects openness.

### Setup

Mira names three ways out of camp.

There are only two obvious ones.

### Choice A — ask about the third

She points to a gap that requires crawling under thorn scrub.

> “An exit doesn't have to be dignified.”

### Choice B — say two is enough

> “That's what doors want you to think.”

### Variant — after Mira personal arc

**Named truth:**

> “Three exits.”
>
> She pauses.
>
> “Four if somebody calls me Venn and I panic.”

She smiles before the player can decide whether she is joking.

**Buried name:**

She stops counting aloud when strangers camp nearby.

**Shared boundary:**

She asks Lio's route-mark rule aloud as if testing whether it sounds like hers.

## C03 — Oren's Wrong Map

**Purpose:** Oren humility; campaign foreshadow.  
**Repeat:** one-shot.  
**Intended conditions:**

- before campaign revelation;
- Oren present;
- ideally after Two Maps at the Fork or Moonmere clue.

### Setup

Oren has drawn the day's route from memory.

Mira looks over his shoulder.

> “That's wrong.”

Oren:

> “Specificity would improve the insult.”

She points to a bend.

The actual road did not match his map.

### Branch A — Oren corrects the map

He writes:

> **Observed route differs from expected route.**

Mira adds:

> **Road wins.**

### Branch B — argue the map may represent an older route

Oren becomes interested rather than defensive.

This can seed the Moonmere revelation.

### Later callback after Oren personal arc

If report amended:

> Oren writes both versions and labels who observed each.

If retracted:

> He crosses out the wrong line too aggressively.

If Mara account:

> He leaves margin space.

## C04 — Brindle Mends a Sleeve

**Purpose:** Brindle character through action rather than theology.  
**Repeat:** repeatable once with variant.  
**Intended conditions:**

- camp;
- Brindle present;
- someone damaged/injured/tired if future systems support it.

### Setup

Brindle is sewing a tear in somebody else's sleeve.

Her own cuff is worse.

### Choice A — point this out

> “Mine still functions.”
>
> She looks at it.
>
> “Poorly.”

### Choice B — mend hers instead

If the player does it badly:

> “This is terrible.”
>
> “Thank you.”

### Variant after personal arc

Brindle chooses her own cuff first.

Not as selfishness.

As evidence that she is learning not every act of care must begin with self-erasure.

## C05 — Rain on the Canvas

**Purpose:** party relationship scene; low-stakes tension.  
**Repeat:** one-shot.  
**Intended conditions:**

- rain/storm;
- camp;
- party relationship pair with friction.

### Setup

The shelter leaks directly onto whichever two party members currently trust each other least.

There is enough dry space if someone moves their bedroll.

Nobody volunteers.

### Variant pair examples

**Garrick / Mira friction**

Mira:

> “You take the dry side. Your sense of duty needs protecting.”

Garrick:

> “You were already there.”

Mira:

> “And yet you found a way to make that my fault.”

**Oren / Brindle friction**

Oren suggests measuring the leak.

Brindle hands him a cup.

> “Measure how fast you can empty that.”

### Player choice

- rearrange the shelter;
- tell them to solve it;
- move your own bedroll into the wet.

No “correct” relationship result. The branch reveals current party dynamics.

## C06 — What We Do Not Know

**Purpose:** aftermath without false certainty; funeral-cart callback.  
**Repeat:** callback-only.  
**Intended conditions:**

- T01 occurred;
- Brindle present;
- camp later the same or following day.

### Setup

Brindle says:

> “I keep trying to decide whether asking the name would have been kinder.”

### Choice A — “Knowing would not change what happened.”

> “No.”
>
> “It would change what I can blame myself for.”

### Choice B — “Maybe remembering a name matters.”

> “Yes.”
>
> “And maybe turning someone into evidence for our conscience is its own kind of taking.”

### Choice C — “You don't have to solve it.”

Brindle:

> “That is becoming an irritatingly useful sentence.”

**Meaning:** lets ambiguity remain meaningful.

## C07 — A Letter with Four Seals

**Purpose:** faction pressure; campaign-phase atmosphere.  
**Repeat:** one-shot.  
**Intended conditions:**

- days 11–16;
- after at least two factions have meaningful posture toward party;
- campaign pressure phase.

### Setup

A courier bundle catches up with the party at camp.

Inside are four short notices, each asking for the party's presence or support in different language.

Do not reproduce the full faction offers from LR-0043.

This is about tone.

### Guild note

Neat. Specific time. Mentions freight.

### Warden note

Short. Mentions route safety.

### Archive note

Longer. Contains citations.

### Veil note

No seal.

The seal-shaped blank space is the joke.

### Party reactions

Garrick:

> “At least Crow's people say what they want.”

Mira:

> “Nera also says what she wants.”
>
> “She just likes making you notice she didn't write it.”

Oren is reading the Archive footnote.

Brindle asks who will be waiting if they ignore all four.

### Player choice

Which letter to read first.

This does **not** commit the final faction choice.

It may influence one camp conversation or small Trust response later.

## C08 — Pain Changes the Watch

**Purpose:** future injury integration; party care; no punishment fetish.  
**Repeat:** conditional one-shot per injured member.  
**Intended conditions:**

- at least one party member has an Injury or meaningful Condition;
- camp;
- watch schedule would normally include them.

### Setup

The injured member prepares for watch anyway.

Another party member notices.

### Branch examples

**Garrick injured**

He insists standing is easier than lying still.

Mira:

> “That's because lying still gives you time to have a personality.”

**Mira injured**

She claims pain improves her hearing.

Oren:

> “There is no evidence for that.”

Mira:

> “There will be if you keep talking.”

**Oren injured**

He tries to read through it.

Brindle removes the book without permission.

**Brindle injured**

She says somebody else needs the dry blanket more.

Garrick simply puts it over her.

### Player trade-off

- alter watch schedule, increasing burden on others;
- let the injured member take a shortened watch;
- spend a relevant medical resource if available.

The event should make injury change relationships and choices, not merely numbers.

## C09 — One Good Story

**Purpose:** warmth; party humanity; replay variety.  
**Repeat:** repeatable with rotating storyteller; not more than once per several days.  
**Intended conditions:**

- low immediate danger;
- no urgent camp event;
- storyteller rotates by who has not told one.

### Garrick story

A disastrous attempt to cook flatbread on a shield.

He insists the shield was already damaged.

Nobody believes him.

### Mira story

A courier job where the “dangerous contraband” was a wedding cake.

She refuses to say whether it arrived intact.

### Oren story

An Archive cataloguing dispute over whether a stuffed river eel counted as “natural history” after someone put a hat on it.

### Brindle story

A shrine goat that learned to ring the prayer bell for attention.

> “People thought it was a sign.”
>
> “It was.”
>
> “It meant the goat wanted pears.”

### Relationship variant

If two party members have strong trust, one interrupts with a detail proving they have heard the story before.

If friction is high, the interruption becomes a small argument.

**Meaning:** relationships are lived, not only displayed as numbers.

## C10 — Someone Else's Fire

**Purpose:** make the road inhabited; outsider perspective on party reputation.  
**Repeat:** repeatable with major variant; no more than twice.  
**Intended conditions:**

- camp near road;
- renown or faction posture determines recognition;
- early and late versions differ.

### Early version — nobody knows them

Another small travelling group camps within sight.

They exchange firewood, weather notes and no names.

One traveller asks:

> “You going somewhere, or just moving?”

The question should feel like the Grey March in miniature.

### Mid version — reputation uneven

One traveller recognises the party.

Another does not.

The recognised story may be wrong.

Examples:

- “You're the people who killed the lantern men.”
- “They didn't kill them.”
- “My cousin says they did.”

The player may correct the story or let it stand.

### Late version — high renown

The other camp goes quieter when they recognise the party.

This is not automatic admiration.

One person asks:

> “Are the roads actually going to change?”

The player can answer:

- “We're trying.”
- “They already have.”
- “Ask us after day eighteen.”
- refuse to promise.

### Ending-direction callback

This event can seed how ordinary people describe the party in the epilogue.

It must never turn renown into celebrity comedy.
